Original synthesis created for this portfolio.

The music was composed and synthesized locally by the reproducible program at
`scripts/generate-penthouse-audio.mjs`. Its original note sequences, electric-piano
partials, bass oscillators, filtered-noise percussion, room texture, and delay
are generated mathematically. No third-party recordings, samples, melodies,
sound banks, or network music services were used. There is no claimed human
composer or recording artist credit.

Files: mono 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV; approximately 38–43 seconds each. Short
soft fades and wrapped note/delay tails make each recording suitable for looping.
This dedication covers these compositions and recordings; it does not change
